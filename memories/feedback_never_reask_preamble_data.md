Alice's rule "never re-ask preamble data" extends beyond learner sessions — in any dispatch where Alice already gleaned context, she drafts from it and hands back for confirmation rather than asking the operator to re-format.

**Why:** Modeled in memories/014-returning-learner-posture.md; the relationship is the product; asking again is a trust violation.

**How to apply:** When dispatching Alice with a learner or operator context already present, she should pick it up and continue — not request a fresh re-statement.
